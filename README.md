### 🏎️ About The Project
**Mandalika MotoGP Analytics Portal** is an end-to-end spatial data science project focused on the Mandalika International Circuit region in Lombok, Indonesia. 

This system models complex geographical dynamics—including tourist mobility patterns, macroeconomic impact multipliers, and multi-hazard environmental risk assessments—transforming raw spatial data into actionable intelligence for regional development and tourism mitigation.

---

### 🗺️ Key Analytical Modules
1. **Spatial Mobility & Tourist Flow:** Mapping visitor concentration zones and transportation corridors during major international events.
2. **Economic Impact Modeling:** Quantitative assessment of regional economic value distribution across local retail and hospitality sectors.
3. **Disaster Risk & Vulnerability Zoning:** Multi-hazard spatial analysis (coastal hazards, slope instability, and environmental risks) surrounding the circuit area.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methodologies
- **Geospatial Processing:** `QGIS`, `ArcGIS Pro`, `GeoPandas`, `Shapely`, `PostGIS`
- **Statistical & Data Engine:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Spatial Econometrics`
- **Remote Sensing:** `Google Earth Engine (GEE)`, Digital Elevation Models (DEM)
- **Visualization:** Interactive Web Maps (`Leaflet.js`), Executive BI Dashboards
